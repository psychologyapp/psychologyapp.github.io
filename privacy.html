Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Aurah is not yet open to the public. This is a pre-launch version and will be replaced by a full policy before launch.

Who we are

Aurah is an online counselling platform based in Kerala, India. Contact: soulmindhub@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address (used to sign in with a one-time code), and the name and phone number you choose to add.
  • Your bookings: which therapist, when, and the session status.
  • Payment results (paid, refunded). Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's own screens and never reach us.
  • For therapists who apply: the professional details and qualifications they submit.

How we use it

Only to run the service: sign you in, arrange and manage sessions, handle payments and refunds, and keep the platform safe. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase (data storage and sign-in, Mumbai region)
  • Razorpay (payments)
  • Brevo (sign-in code emails)
  • Google Meet (video and audio sessions)

Google data

Aurah's server uses Google's Meet API, through Aurah's own Google account only, to create a private meeting room for each confirmed session. It does not access any user's Gmail, Calendar, Drive, contacts or other Google data. Aurah's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your information

A therapist sees only the bookings made with them. The meeting link for a session is shown only to that session's client and therapist, and only after the session is confirmed.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information by writing to soulmindhub@gmail.com.
